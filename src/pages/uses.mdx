## Video Production

### Magewell USB Capture HDMI 4K Pro

I bought this in April 2026 after years of dealing with cheaper capture devices that dropped frames or required driver gymnastics.

It's a USB-C dongle that captures HDMI at 4K60 with 4:4:4 color and passes the signal through to a monitor. Plug-and-play on Mac, no drivers, no configuration. The thing just works.

I use it to capture camera feeds into OBS and route HDMI sources into my Mac without the latency or quality loss I had with older gear. The loop-through means I can monitor the HDMI signal on a separate display while capturing—critical when you're streaming and need to see what you're actually sending.

It's built like a tank. Aluminum housing, good thermal design, proper cable strain relief. The USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 interface delivers the 20Gbps bandwidth needed for clean 4K60 capture without compression artifacts.

Worth the price if you're serious about capture quality. The cheaper options all compromise somewhere—frame drops, color banding, flaky drivers, or no loop-through. This doesn't.
